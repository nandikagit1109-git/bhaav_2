# Demo Video

Place your demo recording here:

- **`bhaav-demo.mp4`** — 10–20 second walkthrough of a Bhaav writing session
  - Record at normal speed; the scroll-scrub component maps scroll position to `currentTime`
  - Shorter videos scrub more smoothly
  - Export as MP4 (H.264) for best browser compatibility
  - Resolution: 1920×1080 or 1280×720

- **`bhaav-poster.jpg`** — A screenshot of the video's first frame
  - Used as a placeholder while the video loads and for `prefers-reduced-motion`
  - JPEG or PNG, under 200KB recommended

The video never auto-plays — users scrub through it by scrolling the section.
